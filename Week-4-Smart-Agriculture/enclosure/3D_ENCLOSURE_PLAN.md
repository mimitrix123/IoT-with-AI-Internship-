# 3D-printed enclosure plan

Design a two-piece IP-rated outdoor enclosure concept with a removable lid, PCB standoffs, cable glands, sensor/pump terminal access and ventilation for the electronics where appropriate.

## Suggested design constraints
- Wall thickness: 2–3 mm
- PCB standoffs: sized to the chosen board mounting holes
- Separate low-voltage pump wiring path from sensor wiring
- OLED viewing window on the lid
- Rubber gasket channel around lid perimeter
- Cable glands for field connections
- Drainage/condensation consideration at the lowest point

Prototype in PETG or another weather-resistant material; validate the actual enclosure against local environmental conditions before outdoor deployment.
